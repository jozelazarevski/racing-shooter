Four values the running game disagrees with
Sky
The reference is under weather. skyTop and skyHorizon on azur and liguria are a clear blue day.
Sea
Follows the sky it reflects. seaColor stays a bright Riviera blue that no cloud could cast.
Asphalt
Dark charcoal with crisp white lines. The town road is a warm grey sett — right for a village, too pale for a Grand Prix street.
Plaza paving
A mauve-rose flag, not the red-brown fringe. It is the largest flat area in the frame, so the hue carries.

Terrace & street kit
Eight parts the frame is furnished with
Heights are in world units so a part can be built straight from the sheet.
01
Fountain
Round basin 3.4 u across, one pedestal, one bowl. It anchors the plaza — nothing else on it is taller than a person.
02
Balustrade
1.1 u to the coping, baluster every 0.62 u. It runs the whole terrace edge — a gap in it reads as damage.
03
Rusticated rampart
Two stone values, courses broken at random, one arched opening. No mortar lines — the block edges do the work.
04
Twin-globe lamp
4.1 u to the globes, arms 1.6 u apart, cast base. Spaced 14 u along the terrace and the pavement both.
05
Palm
Trunk 7 u, six to eight fronds in two greens, crown wider than it is tall. Only on the terrace — never on the hillside.
06
Kerbing & Armco
Red/white blocks 0.9 u long on a 1.8 u pitch, barrier at 0.72 u with a double corrugation. Circuit furniture, town street.
07
Hoarding & cone
Flat red board on two legs, two white bands, no logo. One cone at every kerb transition — the tell that a road is a circuit today.
08
Figures
1.8 u tall, three flat parts — torso, legs, head — one darker edge for the shaded side. No faces, no outlines.

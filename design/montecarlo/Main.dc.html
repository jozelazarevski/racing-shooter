Monte Carlo, overcast
Reference read as a depth stack
six planes · two values each · no gradients
PLANE 1
Overcast sky
Big flat facets, darkest top-left, paling to the horizon. No blue anywhere.
PLANE 2
Ridges & sea
Three ridge bands, each paler than the one in front. Sea borrows the sky's grey.
PLANE 3
Wooded slope
Near-black clumps with one lighter top plane; villas and a campanile scattered through.
PLANE 4
Mid-town stack
One modern slab against pastel blocks — the height break that says Monaco, not village.
PLANE 5
Rampart terrace
Rusticated stone, balustrade, palms, a fountain on mauve paving, twin-globe lamps.
PLANE 6
Street & circuit
Dark asphalt, red/white kerbing, Armco — the race furniture sits inside the town, not beside it.

ONE THUMB — the left pad does everything: sideways steers, up is gas, down is brake.

STEERING

How quickly the car answers the wheel. RELAXED turns slower and is easier to hold straight at speed; SHARP turns harder for the same input — quicker into a hairpin, twitchier everywhere else.

DRIVING AID

A gentle nudge back in line when you are not steering, so the car stops wandering. It never fights your input and never takes a corner for you. PRO off · STANDARD half · ASSIST full.
